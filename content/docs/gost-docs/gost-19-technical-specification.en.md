A technical specification for developing SkladMaster («СкладМастер»), an application for tracking goods at a small warehouse. It covers the program's purpose and scope, requirements for its functions, reliability, hardware, and documentation, cost-efficiency indicators, development stages, and the acceptance procedure.

{{< callout context="note" icon="outline/info-circle" >}}
The document is available in Russian only.
{{< /callout >}}

- **Standard:** GOST 19.201-78 (Unified System for Program Documentation. Technical Specification. Content and Layout Requirements)
- **Document code:** П.Ж.00001 01 ТЗ 01
- **Size:** 13 sheets
